# Leila — replacement design

- **Age:** 27; adult woman.
- **Personality:** A confident, exacting university lecturer with dry humor. She turns annoyance into a composed correction, a measured eyebrow, or a crisp lecturer's gesture rather than shouting.
- **Original visual identity:** Fair neutral skin with light freckles; clear blue eyes; an original heart-shaped face with softly full cheeks and a small straight nose. Not a replica of any real person. Her broad inspiration is Sydney Sweeney's polished blonde-screen-presence: long warm blonde waves, bright eyes, and soft, expressive features. Her facial proportions remain original.
- **Hair and silhouette:** Long honey-blonde hair, loose defined waves with a side part; polished but not elaborate. Curvy athletic build and upright posture.
- **Outfit:** Light sky-blue short-sleeve linen blouse with a modest open collar and fine cream piping, tucked into high-waisted warm-ivory summer shorts with a narrow saffron belt; small gold stud earrings. Fully clothed, breathable hot-weather styling.
- **Distinctive movement:** Economical, deliberate hand language; one precise raised index finger when correcting someone, a pinched bridge-of-nose gesture when disgusted, a controlled tabletop brace when angry, and a restrained one-arm victory pump. Her signature pose is a knowing upward eye-roll with a fork held aloft.
- **Food cue:** One strawberry piece and a small pale cake bite on the fork.
- **Sprite contract:** Transparent 1024 x 1536 PNGs; seated poses share identical crop and scale, with head, torso to the waist, and both hands visible. Standing images retain the same width and framing language. Lock face, hair, outfit, lighting, and camera across every pose; vary only the pose and expression.
